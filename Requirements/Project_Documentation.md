# CS 200 Project 1: ChocAn Requirements

## Project Documentation

This document provides a brief overview of the team's work for **CS 200 Project 1: ChocAn Requirements**.

The team worked together to develop the requirements report, use case descriptions, assumptions, glossary, and use case diagram for the ChocAn system. The project required the team to analyze the given ChocAn requirements and model the system's major interactions and functionality.

### Team Collaboration

The team met **three times** to work together on the project. During these meetings, we discussed the project requirements, divided the work among team members, reviewed our individual sections, and brought the different parts together into the final requirements document.

The completed document represents the work that the team developed and reviewed together for this project.
